Joint modeling and inference of multiple-subject high-dimensional sparse vector autoregressive models

high Younghoon Kim, Zachary F. Fisher, Vladas Pipiras · stat.ME · 27 Oct 2026
Proposes a communication‑efficient joint estimation method for multiple‑subject sparse vector autoregressive models with varying convergence rates.

Abstract

The multiple-subject vector autoregression (multi-VAR) model captures heterogeneous temporal dependence across subjects through commonly shared and subject-unique paths (entries) of individual (subject-specific) sparse VAR transition matrices. Available joint penalized regression approaches suffer from statistical inefficiency, whereby the convergence rates of the individual estimators are determined by a subject with the largest sparsity level. An enhanced multi-VAR estimation approach is proposed based on a communication-efficient data integration framework and a suitable identifiability condition in meta-analysis. It is shown that component-wise different convergence rates can be achieved. A hypothesis testing framework is also developed to assess the significance of common paths, as well as the homogeneity of subject-specific paths. Furthermore, a multiple hypothesis test to control the false discovery rate is suggested within a subregion of interest among the entire paths. A simulation study and a real data application compare the performance of the considered methods to other approaches.
