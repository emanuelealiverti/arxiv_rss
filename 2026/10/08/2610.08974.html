Projected Normal Models on Recursively Curved Tori

low Surojit Biswas, Aron Persson · stat.ME, stat.TH · 8 Oct 2026
Develops a statistical framework for angular data on recursively curved tori using projected normal distributions.

Abstract

We develop a computationally tractable statistical framework for angular data supported on a recursively embedded curved torus $\mathbb{T}^n\hookrightarrow\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$. Due to the recursive structure we build an exact uniform sampler by generalizing the process from the curved 2-torus. We generalize projected normal distributions to the curved $n$-torus and we develop inference theory for these distributions. We utilize both maximum-log-likelihood estimates and energy-based Fr'echet estimates for the projected normal distribution for this purpose. This is done by establishing identifiability for isotropic projected normal distributions and by applying theory of maximum likelihood and intrinsic statistics for the curved tori. We numerically verify the geometry, sampler, density transformation, and energy solver. Lastly, we demonstrate the applicability of the proposed model using real data.
