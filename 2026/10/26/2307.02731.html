Visualization Supporting Talent Portrait Generation:an Empirical Study

low Yuqing Fan, Shenghui Cheng · stat.AP · 26 Oct 2026
This paper proposes a framework for visualizing researchers' academic trajectories and successes.

Abstract

In current era of scientific and technological advancements,the importance of talent resources is increasingly highlighted.This article will attempt to summarize the academic trajectories and successes of numerous researchers from both past and present,aiming to reproduce the correlation between their personal development and academic output.This article proposes a framework of academic data collection,then use the framework to visualize numerous scientists research accomplishments,collaborative partners,and research inheritance.The article additionally posited a range of scholarly indices designed to quantitatively assess the impact of scientists.
