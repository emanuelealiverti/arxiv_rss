Nesting or resolution? Facility proxies for variance estimation versus confounding adjustment in claims data without facility identifiers

low Yuta Nonomiya, Tomoka Matsuura, Ryota Kawai, Andreas Dengel, Koichi Kise, Wakaba Fukushima, Ayumi Shintani · stat.ME, stat.AP · 20 Oct 2026
Researchers develop facility proxies for variance estimation and confounding adjustment in claims data without facility identifiers.

Abstract

In claims databases, facility identifiers are often removed during de-identification, leaving only coarse attributes such as bed size. Yet facility information is needed for variance estimation, confounding adjustment and assessment of treatment availability. We examined whether accreditation-gated billing items can build strata standing for hospitals, and how to construct a facility proxy for each use. In 1,730,736 adults undergoing surgery (Medical Data Vision database, Japan, 2019-2023), we built marker-pattern strata from 66 accreditation-gated receipt codes (markers) and evaluated each grouping against a true facility identifier in an insurer-based database (DeSC Healthcare) and in simulation. The variance explained by the marker-pattern strata decreased with the extent to which a variable was patient- rather than hospital-determined ($R^2$ 0.921 for an accreditation item, 0.036 for sex). For variance estimation, the supplied-attribute cells, which can merge but never split hospitals, kept the type I error rate at 4.8% with a wild cluster bootstrap, against 5.4% for the true identifier and 39% when clustering was ignored; every finer marker-based grouping did worse. For adjustment, evaluated with real facility-level confounders and simulated null exposures, the marker pattern removed 51% of the confounding related to accreditation-linked facility characteristics, against 8% for the cells. The proportion of patients in groups mixing hospitals with and without the relevant accreditation was 49% for the marker pattern against 98% for the cells. No single facility proxy serves every purpose: nesting governs variance estimation, and resolution governs adjustment and treatment availability. Without a facility identifier, the supplied-attribute cells can carry the standard errors, and marker-pattern strata are worth considering for the other two uses.
