Optimal Neural Estimation for High-Dimensional Nonparametric Regression under Multisource Covariate Shift

medium Chenghao Xu, Haotian Xu, Shivam Kumar, Daren Wang · stat.ME, stat.ML, stat.TH · 30 Oct 2026
Proposes an oracle weighted neural estimator with minimax optimal rates for high-dimensional nonparametric regression under multisource covariate shift.

Abstract

We study high-dimensional nonparametric regression under multisource covariate shift, where $\mathrm J$ sources of training data share a common regression function but differ in their covariate distributions, sample sizes, and noise variances. Our goal is to combine these sources to estimate the regression function accurately under a target covariate distribution. We propose an oracle weighted neural estimator and establish its minimax optimality up to logarithmic factors through matching upper and lower bounds. The resulting rate is $\mathsf n_{\rm eff}^{-2p/(2p+K)}$, where $p$ and $K$ denote the effective smoothness and dimension of the regression function, and the effective sample size is \begin{align} \mathsf n_{\rm eff} =\sum_{j=1}^{\mathrm J} \frac{n_j}{(1+\sigma_{\rho,j})\sigma_{\epsilon,j}^{2}}. \nonumber \end{align} Here, $n_j$ is the sample size of source $j$, $\sigma_{\epsilon,j}^{2}$ is its noise variance, and $\sigma_{\rho,j}$ controls the deviation between the covariate distribution of source $j$ and the target covariate distribution. We also develop an adaptive procedure that requires no knowledge of source noise variances or covariate-shift parameters. Under suitable conditions, this procedure attains the oracle minimax rate up to logarithmic factors.
