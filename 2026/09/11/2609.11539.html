Extended One-Liners for the Gamma, Poisson, and Binomial Distributions

low Dylan Greaves · stat.CO · 11 Sep 2026
Explicit transformations of three distributions using elementary operations are demonstrated.

Abstract

We demonstrate explicit transformations of three independent uniforms with exact Gamma$(a,1)$, $a>0$, Poisson$(\lambda)$, $\lambda>0$, and Binomial$(N,p)$, $N\geq 1$, $0\leq p \leq 1$ laws using only elementary operations.
