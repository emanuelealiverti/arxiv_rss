Bayesian inference on the exponentiated Kumaraswamy distribution with applications

low Ziqian Sun, Miguel A. Juárez · stat.ME · 21 Jul 2026
The paper discusses Bayesian estimation of the exponentiated Kumaraswamy distribution using HMC.

Abstract

We discuss Bayesian estimation of the exponentiated Kumaraswamy distribution, based on a scale-location parameterisation. The parameterisation facilitates prior elicitation and interpretation of results, but potentially entails identifiability issues that are addressed trough a hierarchical weak informative prior setting. Our HMC implementation enables off-the-shelf utility for practitioners, and is illustrated on synthetic and real data.
