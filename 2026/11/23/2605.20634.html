Random weighting inference for linear regression under serial dependence of unknown form

low Mous-Abou Hamadou, Martial Longla, Mathias Nthiani Muia, Mahmud Hasan · stat.ME, stat.TH · 23 Nov 2026
Develops randomly weighted least-squares confidence regions for regression coefficients under unspecified short- or long-memory serial dependence.

Abstract

We construct confidence regions for a linear regression’s coefficients, with regressors and errors forming a stationary ergodic sequence of serial dependence not necessarily known. We weight the observations by random variables from an independent auxiliary sample and estimate the coefficients by weighted least squares. We prove the estimator to be asymptotically normal with the sandwich covariance if and only if the bandwidth shrinks fast enough for the weights’ randomness to dominate the score’s serial dependence. Wald regions have the nominal level, under short memory with the dependence left unspecified and under long memory with a non-normal least-squares limit. We derive a condition specific to the slopes and a bandwidth from consistent memory-order estimates, and identify the estimator asymptotically with least squares perturbed by independent noise. In simulations, we compare our regions with self-normalized, fixed-bandwidth, and resampled intervals under short and long memory, and we apply the method to daily wind speeds at Irish stations, a long-memory series.
