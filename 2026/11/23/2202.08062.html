Where the Model Frequently Meets the Road: Combining Statistical, Formal, and Case Study Methods

low Andrew Bennett, Bear F. Braumoeller, John Severini · stat.ME · 23 Nov 2026
Analyzes default assumptions in formal, statistical, and case study research and offers guidance for combining these methods.

Abstract

This paper briefly analyzes the working or default assumptions researchers in the formal, statistical, and case study traditions typically hold regarding the sources of unexplained variance, parameter values, functional forms, time, and external validity. We argue that these working assumptions are often not essential to each method, and that these assumptions can be relaxed in ways that allow multimethod work to proceed. We then analyze the comparative advantages of different combinations of formal, statistical, and case study methods, and offer advice on how to combine these methods.
