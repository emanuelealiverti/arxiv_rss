Renewable Estimation for Generalized Nonparametric Regression with Dynamic Spline Bases and Likelihood Pullback

low Houtian Yi, Zhaolun Wang, Xin Zhao · stat.ME · 23 Nov 2026

Abstract

A fixed spline space may limit approximation accuracy in generalized nonparametric regression with streaming data. We propose a renewable penalized-likelihood method for generalized nonparametric regression with canonical exponential-family responses. Our method combines dynamic cubic-spline refinement with Gram-metric pullback of historical score and curvature summaries. The pullback preserves the retained historical likelihood quadratic on the embedded previous spline space, while incoming data update the fit and its summaries. Under regularity and algorithmic conditions, including a growing startup and an uncapped refinement regime, we establish $L^2$-consistency at rate $O_p(N^{-2/5})$ for a fixed target in $H^2[0,1]$ and a smaller-order energy-norm discrepancy from a matched offline estimator. Simulations and applications to the Voice and Catalina datasets show that dynamic bases improve estimation when the initial representation is restrictive, with additional gains from pullback. A preliminary computational comparison also shows lower computational and storage costs than the offline methods.
