Regularized trimmed Factorial and Reduced K-means for contaminated high-dimensional data: separating geometry, search and selection

low Cinzia Di Nuzzo, Matteo Farnè · stat.ME, stat.AP · 13 Nov 2026
Introduces regularized trimmed factorial and reduced k-means for robust high-dimensional clustering.

Abstract

Factorial and Reduced K-means estimate a partition and a low-dimensional clustering subspace jointly, which makes them attractive when the variables that separate groups are not those that dominate total variance. On contaminated high-dimensional data they face three linked problems: trimming can make the scatter matrices singular, the criterion has many local optima, and the rank, number of groups and trimming proportion are unknown and interact. We propose REG-HD-TFKM and REG-HD-TRKM, regularized trimmed versions of the two methods, together with a selection procedure that handles these problems as separate decisions. A ridge-stabilized spectral step keeps every fit well defined; heterogeneous multistart search explores alternative solutions; method-specific agreement and reproducibility scores choose among them; and trimming is applied only when a radial diagnostic or the path over trimming levels supports it. A simulation study with 41 design cells shows why the separation matters. Two representations of euro-area bank balance sheets show that similar partitions can rest on nearly orthogonal subspaces, and that the same selected structure can describe different retained samples.
