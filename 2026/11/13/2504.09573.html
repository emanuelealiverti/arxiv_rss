A grid-based methodology for fast online changepoint detection

low Per August Jarval Moen · stat.ME · 13 Nov 2026

Abstract

We propose a grid-based methodology that turns suitable offline (non-sequential) changepoint tests into online (sequential) procedures by testing for changes over a dynamically updating grid of candidate locations. For a broad class of test statistics, including those based on empirical averages and certain likelihood ratios, we show that the resulting online procedure has update and storage costs that grow at most logarithmically with the sample size. We further show that the resulting procedure has explicit control of the false alarm probability, and that finite-sample power guarantees for the test translate directly into non-asymptotic upper bounds on the detection delay, provided the test is robust to constant-factor misspecification of the changepoint location. We illustrate the methodology by constructing online detectors for changes in the mean and in the covariance matrix of multivariate data, and prove that their detection delays are near-optimal. The effectiveness of the methodology is supported by a simulation study, where we compare its performance in detecting mean changes with that of state-of-the-art online methods. To illustrate its practical applicability, we use the methodology to detect structural changes in currency exchange rates in real time.
