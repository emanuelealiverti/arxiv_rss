Location--Scale Calibration for Generalized Posteriors

high Shu Tamano, Yui Tomo · stat.ME · 13 Nov 2026
Calibrates generalized posterior draws to achieve asymptotically invariant uncertainty quantification under learning rate variations.

Abstract

General Bayesian updating replaces the likelihood with a loss scaled by a learning rate, but posterior uncertainty can depend sharply on that scale. We propose a simple post-processing that aligns generalized posterior draws with the asymptotic sampling distribution of a penalized estimator, yielding uncertainty quantification that is asymptotically invariant to the learning rate. We prove a total-variation normal approximation at an effective scale determined by a score central limit theorem, allowing sample-size-dependent prior factors, non-i.i.d. observations, and locally smooth convex penalties under model misspecification. Within this framework, we justify the open-faced sandwich adjustment (Shaby, 2014) and extend it from covariance rescaling to a location–scale calibration whose draws converge in total variation to the target for any learning rate. In our empirical illustration, calibrated draws maintain stable coverage, interval width, and bias over orders of magnitude in the learning rate and closely track frequentist benchmarks, whereas uncalibrated posteriors vary markedly.
