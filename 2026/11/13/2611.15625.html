Robust Tests for Heteroskedasticity in Nonparametric Regression under Covariate-Dependent Higher-Order Moments

medium Yongzhen Feng, Xiaojun Song · stat.ME · 13 Nov 2026
Proposes Cramér–von Mises and Kolmogorov–Smirnov tests for heteroskedasticity in nonparametric regression using third‑order U‑processes.

Abstract

We propose Cram'er–von Mises- and Kolmogorov–Smirnov-type tests of constant conditional variance in nonparametric regression. The test statistics are constructed from a third-order $U$-process and remain valid when higher-order features of the conditional error distribution vary with the covariates. We derive their limiting distributions under the null hypothesis and study their asymptotic behavior under fixed alternatives and Pitman-type local alternatives. A multiplier bootstrap procedure calibrates the critical values, and Monte Carlo experiments show that the proposed tests have rejection frequencies close to the nominal level in the simulation designs. An application to the National Child Development Study provides evidence of heteroskedasticity in pooled weekly earnings. In the male hourly-earnings sample, rejecting a stronger error-distribution restriction does not entail rejecting homoskedasticity.
