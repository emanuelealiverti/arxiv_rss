Stable and efficient covariance estimation for sample L-moments using Hájek projections

medium Jihong Park, Palakorn Seenoi, Jeong-Soo Park · stat.ME · 2 Nov 2026
Provides positive‑semidefinite covariance estimators for sample L‑moments with faster rank‑prefix algorithms.

Abstract

L-moments are useful summary statistics and are widely used in statistical analysis. The joint covariance matrix of sample L-moments not only describes their sampling uncertainty but is also needed for covariance-weighted distances and related statistical procedures. An exact unbiased covariance estimator is already available. However, in our previous applications, this estimator sometimes produced singular or indefinite covariance matrices. Such matrices cause problems when matrix inversion is required. The present paper addresses this difficulty. We examine standard positive-definite corrections and two covariance estimators based on leave-one-out sample L-moments: the delete-one jackknife estimator and an estimator based on the H'ajek projection. Both are positive semidefinite by construction. Simulation results show that the projection estimator generally has smaller estimation error and better numerical stability than the exact unbiased estimator. The jackknife estimator overestimates the variances of higher-order sample L-moments and has larger estimation error than the projection estimator. We also develop fast rank-prefix algorithms for the three covariance estimators considered. These algorithms substantially reduce computation time.
