Fuzzy entropic triple k-means

medium Mariaelena Bottazzi Schenone, Roberto Rocci, Maurizio Vichi · stat.ME · 2 Nov 2026
Proposes fuzzy entropic triple k-means for clustering three-way data arrays via entropy-regularized fuzzy membership and block centroids.

Abstract

This paper proposes fuzzy entropic triple k-means (FE3KM), an entropy-regularized fuzzy partitioning method for three-way data arrays that simultaneously clusters objects, variables, and occasions. The method combines three row-stochastic fuzzy membership matrices and a full array of block centroids through a product-weighted squared-distance criterion with mode-specific entropy regularization to represent uncertainty in cluster assignments. With normalized product membership weights and weighted-mean block centroids, the ordinary total sum of squares decomposes exactly into within- and between-block components, further attributable to each mode and to individual clusters within each. This identity supports cluster-level descriptive summaries and conditional assessments of the gain associated with each way’s partition. We derive a block-coordinate descent algorithm with weighted-mean centroid updates and entropy-based membership updates, and clarify the relationship with tandem procedures, independent partitioning, hard three-way partitioning, and Tucker reconstruction. An accelerated variant is also proposed for large arrays. A simulation study examines partition and centroid recovery under different noise levels and array sizes, sensitivity to the entropy regularization parameters, and a comparison with tandem and independent procedures. An application to a benchmark three-way TV ratings dataset shows how FE3KM illustrates interpretable object-variable-occasion structures and quantifies the contribution of each cluster and mode to overall data variability.
