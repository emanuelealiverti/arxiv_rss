Statistical inference for quantile correlation regression with bivariate outcomes

medium Feipeng Zhang, Heng Peng · stat.ME · 2 Nov 2026
Proposes a GLM for covariate effects on the quantile correlation coefficient of bivariate outcomes.

Abstract

It is increasing scientifically important to recognize, quantify and visualize the dependence structure between the bivariate outcomes. In this paper, we investigate conditional dependence using a new dependence measure, the quantile correlation coefficient, which is defined by the Pearson correlation of two quantile-hits of bivariate outcomes. Furthermore, we propose a new regression model for covariate effects directly on the quantile correlation coefficient via a generalized linear regression model. The proposed model has intuitive interpretation and provides a comprehensive view of covariate effects across quantiles of bivariate outcomes. The proposed estimator enjoys desirable asymptotic properties including uniform consistency and weak convergence as a process in the quantile index. Additionally, we develop the general hypothesis testing for the regression coefficients, with applications to summarize covariate effects across the quantile ranges. Simulation studies demonstrate the good finite sample performance of the proposed estimation and inference procedures. Two empirical applications of the proposed method reveal interesting insights in premature twins data for measuring association and ChIP-seq data for measuring reproducibility.
