Personalized Federated Vector Autoregression with Personalization Diversity

medium Zhiyun Fan, Xiaoyu Zhang, Guodong Li, Di Wang · stat.ME · 16 Nov 2026
Introduces personalized federated VAR with shared low-rank dynamics and personalization diversity, providing theoretical convergence guarantees.

Abstract

Personalized federated estimation of high-dimensional time series faces two challenges: identifying shared dynamics and retaining the statistical gains from pooling. We address these challenges through personalization diversity in a vector autoregressive model with shared low-rank dynamics, shared sparse links, and personalized sparse departures. A cross-client support-frequency cap yields a sharp majority threshold for shared-personalized identification, while an additional cross-client $\ell_q$ condition captures approximate diversity and controls approximation under a smaller working personalization frequency cap. We develop PerFeCT-VAR, Personalized Federated Vector Autoregression via Frequency-Capped Thresholding, which jointly enforces clientwise sparsity and cross-client frequency constraints. Under suitable sampling and cross-component compatibility conditions, we establish initialization guarantees and joint local linear convergence for heterogeneous stationary client distributions. Our componentwise error bounds show that diversity attenuates both estimation and approximation errors transmitted from personalized to shared components, and quantify the tradeoff induced by the working cap. These bounds identify regimes in which the shared components attain pooled accuracy despite non-vanishing personalized effects, while personalized estimation achieves local-sample accuracy on average. Simulations and an application to multistore retail revenue data illustrate the estimation, forecasting, and interpretive benefits of the proposed framework.
