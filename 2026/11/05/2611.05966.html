Prior-data fitted networks for variable selection

high Omar Melikechi · stat.ME, stat.ML · 5 Nov 2026
PFNs estimate posterior inclusion probabilities for variable selection in Bayesian networks without posterior sampling.

Abstract

Prior-data fitted networks (PFNs) have become state-of-the-art for tabular prediction but remain underexplored in other areas of statistics. In this work, we present two PFNs for Bayesian variable selection: CD-PFN identifies conditional dependencies, and PC-PFN identifies parents and children in a causal graph. Both networks estimate posterior inclusion probabilities (PIPs) without posterior samples or data-specific modeling. In semi-synthetic and real data experiments, CD-PFN and PC-PFN often outperform existing methods, demonstrating the value of PFNs for variable selection and their potential in statistics more broadly. Open-source software is available.
