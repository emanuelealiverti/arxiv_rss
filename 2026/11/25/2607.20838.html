A Markov duration model with power-law decay via level-slope matching

medium Kyungsub Lee · stat.ME · 25 Nov 2026
Develops a two-dimensional Markov duration model with power-law decay, proves positive recurrence and geometric ergodicity, and applies it to financial data.

Abstract

We develop a two-dimensional Markov duration model with power-law rate decay and a flexible residual distribution. The two state variables specify the excitation level and decay scale of a single power-law rate curve, and each update matches the level and slope jumps of a power-law Hawkes intensity. Under a sufficient stability condition, a finite residual mean, and a positive continuous residual density, the state at event times is positive Harris recurrent and converges geometrically in total variation to a unique invariant distribution. With unit exponential residuals, the sufficient region contains every parameter value for which the matched Hawkes process is subcritical. We apply the model to E-mini S\&P 500 futures and NVDA midquote durations at several price-change thresholds. Flexible residual laws improve likelihood-based fit, and power-law decay lowers one-step negative log predictive scores relative to exponential decay. An autoregressive conditional duration model generally leaves less residual autocorrelation, while forecast rankings vary with the price threshold and evaluation criterion.
