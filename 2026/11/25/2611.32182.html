Asymptotically Efficient Conformal Inference for Individual Treatment Effects under Multivalued Treatments

low Lequn Ge, Gaorong Li, Hui Zhao · stat.ME · 25 Nov 2026
Proposes adaptive conformal prediction sets for counterfactual outcomes under multivalued treatments using kernel density-ratio estimation and highest predictive density scores.

Abstract

Most existing conformal inference methods for individual treatment effects are limited to settings with binary treatments. For multiple treatments, propensity score-derived weights often suffer from statistical instability, while contiguous prediction intervals may span low-density regions between modes of potential outcome distributions, leading to inefficient prediction sets. We propose adaptive prediction sets for counterfactual outcomes under multivalued treatments by combining KuLSIF-based density ratio estimation with highest predictive density (HPD) conformal scores derived from conditional density estimates. Both estimators share a reproducing kernel Hilbert space least-squares formulation, which simplifies theoretical derivation and analysis. The resulting prediction sets are robust to heteroscedasticity and multimodal outcome distributions and effectively eliminate low-density regions between distribution modes. Under unconfoundedness assumption and regularity conditions, we prove that the proposed method converges to optimal HPD sets under covariate shift and establish its asymptotic conditional validity and statistical efficiency. Simulation studies and ACIC benchmarks demonstrate that the proposed method achieves empirical coverage probabilities close to the nominal level, while yielding shorter average interval lengths than conventional quantile-based conformal methods for multimodal outcome data.
