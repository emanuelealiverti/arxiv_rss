Optimal Transport Rank Regression for Robust Multivariate-Response Regression

medium Yizhe Ding, Yifan Jiang, Runze Li, Lingzhou Xue · stat.ME · 20 Nov 2026
Introduces optimal transport rank regression for robust multivariate-response regression, providing theory and deep learning implementation.

Abstract

In this paper, we propose optimal transport rank regression for robust multivariate-response mean regression. The proposed method maps residual distributions to the uniform measure on the unit ball and constructs its loss from the resulting optimal transport ranks. This construction exploits cross-response dependence to improve efficiency while preserving population Fisher consistency and orthogonal invariance. Beyond these population properties, we establish statistical guarantees for the proposed method in both linear and nonparametric regression. For linear models, we establish asymptotic normality, characterise robustness, and develop orthogonally invariant asymptotic inference. We derive a distribution-uniform lower bound on asymptotic efficiency relative to least-squares and demonstrate efficiency gains over coordinatewise methods under several heavy-tailed and contaminated distributions. For nonparametric regression, we establish non-asymptotic (L^2(P)) convergence rates and robustness guarantees modulo additive constants using deep neural networks under possibly asymmetric heavy-tailed noise, attaining the minimax-optimal rate up to logarithmic factors. Numerical experiments demonstrate improvements in estimation accuracy and inferential efficiency over coordinatewise and radial robust methods, alongside stability under orthogonal transformations of the responses. An oral microbiome application further shows that the proposed method identifies more associations that are significant in all examined orthonormal response representations compared with each competing method.
