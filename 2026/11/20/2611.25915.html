General Methodology for Asymptotically Balanced Multiple Tests

low Merle Munko · stat.ME · 20 Nov 2026
Develops a general framework for asymptotically balanced multiple tests controlling strong FWER using resampling.

Abstract

In statistical applications, often more than one hypothesis is of interest and, thus, multiple testing problems occur naturally. To control the family-wise error rate, popular adjustment methods, like the Bonferroni and Holm correction, have been developed. However, these methods do not use the dependence structure of the test statistics. Consequently, they are conservative in most settings. To increase the power of the tests, the joint dependence structure of the test statistics can be incorporated for the construction of critical values, for example, by using resampling schemes as bootstrap procedures to approximate the joint distribution. This has already been applied in several contexts. In this work, we develop a very flexible and general theoretical foundation, which can be used to construct asymptotically balanced multiple tests that control the family-wise error rate in the strong sense. Additionally, we prove in the general setting that consistent resampling schemes satisfy the needed conditions.
