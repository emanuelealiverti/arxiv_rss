Cluster-weighted modeling for count data with excess zeros and mixed-type covariates

high Jongik Park, Geoffrey J. McLachlan, Sangkon Oh · stat.ME · 11 Nov 2026
Proposes a zero-inflated negative binomial cluster-weighted model for count data with mixed covariates and excess zeros.

Abstract

Count data may exhibit excess zeros, within-group overdispersion, and heterogeneous relationships between the response and its covariates. Conventional zero-inflated regression models typically impose a single count-regression structure, whereas Poisson count components cannot accommodate residual within-component overdispersion. We propose a zero-inflated negative binomial cluster-weighted model that combines a structural-zero component with multiple negative binomial regression components and jointly models the response and mixed-type covariates.We establish sufficient conditions for identifiability of the proposed model and its Poisson counterpart and develop an expectation-maximization algorithm for estimation. Numerical studies demonstrate improved regression coefficient estimation and clustering performance relative to the Poisson counterpart under within-component overdispersion.
