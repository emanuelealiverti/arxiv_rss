Adaptive local Fréchet regression with polyspherical predictors

low Chang Jun Im · stat.ME, stat.TH · 11 Nov 2026

Abstract

We study local Fr'echet regression for responses taking values in a metric space and predictors on a product of spheres of possibly different dimensions. We propose local constant and local linear estimators with factor-specific bandwidths and establish uniform convergence rates. We select sphere factors by comparing cross-fitted prediction risks for the full and leave-one-factor-out models. The procedure consistently recovers the active factor set, and the selected local constant and local linear estimators attain the same uniform convergence rates as if the active factor set were known. For univariate Wasserstein responses supported on a common compact interval, the uniform rate holds without an entropy condition on the response space. Simulations with scalar and spherical responses and analyses of vessel speeds and drifter speed distributions illustrate the methods.
