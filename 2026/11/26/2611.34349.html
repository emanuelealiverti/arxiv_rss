Guarded Readmission Weighting for Robust Single-Index Frechet Regression

medium Fatma Sevinc Kurnaz · stat.ME, stat.CO · 26 Nov 2026
Proposes guarded readmission weighting for single-index Fréchet regression, robustly downweighting outliers and contaminated leverage points in metric-space responses.

Abstract

Single-index Frechet regression reduces the predictor dimension for metric-space responses such as distributions, networks or curves, but its estimators are sensitive to outliers. We propose guarded readmission weighting (GRW), a one-step computation of case weights from the predictors and the response distances, usable with any weighted estimator. GRW starts from a high-breakdown fit to a multidimensional scaling surrogate, readmits cases by an add-one metric Cook’s distance, downweights remote predictors and keeps a response isolation factor in the final weights. If fewer than $\lfloor n/2\rfloor$ of the $n$ cases are replaced, objects far from the uncontaminated responses receive weight zero; under elliptical predictors, weights depending on the predictors only through the index and the Mahalanobis distance keep the population weighted directions in the index subspace. In simulations, three GRW-weighted estimators keep the mean projection distance to the true direction below about 0.5 under up to 30% scattered and 20% clustered bad leverage points, whereas deletion and $\alpha$-distance covariance become much less accurate as contamination grows; $\alpha$-distance covariance is more accurate in most clean, shape-outlier and good-leverage settings. In two applications, under planted contamination, the weighted surrogate-assisted and least squares directions move less than those of methods without GRW.
