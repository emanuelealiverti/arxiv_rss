New fast and accurate estimators of the five-parameter lambda distribution (FPLD) with practical applications

low Dilanka S. Dedduwakumara, Luke A. Prendergast · stat.ME · 26 Nov 2026
Introduces fast, accurate estimators for the five-parameter lambda distribution, with simulations, applications, an R package, and a web tool.

Abstract

When fitting parametric densities, simple distributions are often chosen because they are computationally efficient to estimate and easy to interpret. However, simplicity can result in important features of the data being missed. This paper introduces fast and reliable estimators of the five-parameter lambda distribution (FPLD). The FPLD is highly flexible, can take many different shapes, and provides greater control over skewness and tail behaviour than distributions with fewer parameters. Simulations are provided to demonstrate the excellent performance of the proposed estimators compared with existing methods, at a fraction of the computational cost. Applications in which speed and accuracy are important are also provided, with comparisons to commonly used but potentially overly simplistic approaches. Finally, an R package implementing the proposed estimators and a web application that allows users to determine the FPLD parameters required to approximate well-known distributions are provided.
