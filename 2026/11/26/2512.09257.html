Debiased Inference via Posterior Corrections for High-Dimensional Regression

high Qihui Chen, Zheng Fang, Ruixuan Liu · stat.CO, stat.ME, stat.ML, stat.TH · 26 Nov 2026
Introduces posterior corrections that debias high-dimensional Bayesian regression posteriors, with Bernstein-von Mises theorems ensuring frequentist validity of credible sets.

Abstract

There has been significant progress in Bayesian inference based on sparsity-inducing priors, such as spike-and-slab and horseshoe priors, for high-dimensional regression. The resulting posteriors, however, in general do not possess desirable frequentist properties, and the credible sets thus cannot serve as valid confidence sets even asymptotically. We introduce a novel debiasing approach that corrects the bias for the entire (possibly approximate) Bayesian posterior distribution. We establish new Bernstein-von Mises theorems that guarantee the frequentist validity of the debiased law. We demonstrate the practical performance of our proposal through Monte Carlo simulations and two empirical applications in economics.
