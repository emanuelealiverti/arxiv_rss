Design-Conditional Prior Elicitation for Dirichlet Process Mixtures: A Unified Framework for Cluster Counts and Weight Control

medium JoonHo Lee · stat.ME, stat.AP, stat.CO · 26 Nov 2026
Calibrates Gamma hyperpriors for Dirichlet process mixture concentration from expected cluster counts, with diagnostics reconciling cluster-count and weight judgments.

Abstract

Dirichlet process mixtures (DPMs) model variation in effects across sites or studies and in latent traits among examinees. The concentration parameter affects both the number of clusters in the sample and the population weights, yet its hyperprior is often chosen by default. We describe a method for choosing this hyperprior when the number of units is fixed by design. We calibrate a Gamma hyperprior to the analyst’s expected cluster count and uncertainty about it, then examine the implied weights. If count and weight judgments conflict, Dual-Anchor selects a compromise and reports its implications for both. Across our simulations and applications, the choice of prior could change the inferred number of clusters considerably even when individual estimates changed little. A higher cluster count under an alternative prior warrants a closer look at the upper tail, where estimates can shift even when the median remains stable. The DPprior R package implements the calibration and diagnostics.
