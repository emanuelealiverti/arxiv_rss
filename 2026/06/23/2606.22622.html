A New Algorithm for Totally Positive Approximations

low Lutz Duembgen, Philip Stange · stat.CO · 23 Jun 2026
This paper proposes a new algorithm for approximating bivariate distributions with finite support, but it does not appear to be directly related to the researcher's core topics in Bayesian methodology and computation.

Abstract

We revisit the problem of approximating a bivariate distribution with finite support by another such distribution which is totally positive or order two (TP2). Approximation is meant in a maximum likelihood sense.
