How to verify that a given process is a Lévy-Driven Ornstein-Uhlenbeck Process


Ibrahim Abdelrazeq , Hardy Smith , Dinmukhammed Zhanbyrshy
[stat.AP]

Assuming that a L'evy-Driven Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (or CAR(1)) processes is observed at discrete times $0$, $h$, $2h$,$\cdots$ $[T/h]h$. Here we introduced a step-by-step methodological approach on how a person would verify the model assumptions supported by real-life data examples using this methodology. The model parameter needs to be estimated and the driving process must be approximated. Approximated increments with estimated parameter of the driving process are used to test the assumptions that the CAR(1) process is L'evy-driven. Performance of the test is illustrated through simulation.
