A statistical test for network similarity


Pierre Miasnikof, Alexander Y. Shetopaloff
[stat.AP]

In this article, we revisit and expand our prior work on graph similarity. In this version of our work, we offer an extensive array of empirical tests. We also examine the sensitivity of our test to network variations. Our test performs exactly as expected, on synthetic and real-world graphs. It offers a very accurate measure of graph (dis)similarity.
