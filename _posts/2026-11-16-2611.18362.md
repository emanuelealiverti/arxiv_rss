In this paper we study the asymptotic properties of an autoregressive model of order p (AR for short), possibly non-centered, whose parameters switch according to an unobserved Markov chain. Departing from the conventional assumption of independent innovations, we merely require the errors to be uncorrelated but not necessarily independent, thereby widening substantially the range of potential applications of Markov-switching AR models. We establish sufficient conditions for the consistency and asymptotic normality of the proposed moment estimators. We also propose a consistent estimator of the asymptotic variance matrix of the moment estimators. The finite-sample performance of the proposed procedures is illustrated through Monte Carlo experiments and an empirical study based on hourly meteorological data, which together provide strong support for the theoretical findings.
