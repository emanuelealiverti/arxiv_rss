Genome-wide 5-hydroxymethylcytosine (5hmC) profiles of circulating cell-free DNA (cfDNA) can reveal epigenetic changes associated with multiple myeloma (MM), but their analysis requires adjusting for known risk factors while selecting a few informative genes from thousands. We propose semiparametric probit Bayesian Additive Regression Tree (spBART), in which the covariates enter a linear component with interpretable coefficients and the genes enter a sparse tree ensemble whose posterior inclusion probabilities select genes conditional on the covariates. The analysis selects eight candidate loci, \textit{IL1RAP}, \textit{ST5}, \textit{HERC6}, \textit{KL}, \textit{MYO1E}, \textit{ELK3}, \textit{CAPN2} and \textit{UBR4}. Selection of \textit{IL1RAP} connects the circulating signature to the \textit{IL-1/IL-6} signaling axis implicated in MM. The final model achieves an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve of 0.96 and a Brier score of 0.09 in the held-out validation set. Our simulation studies support the modeling strategy when adjustment variables act approximately linearly, but false discoveries exceed the nominal target in application-sized settings with correlated features. Overall, the proposed model identifies a compact set of circulating epigenetic signatures that are highly associated with MM.
