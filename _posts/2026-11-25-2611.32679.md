This paper proposes an adaptive multi-kernel correntropy Kalman filter (AMCKF) for robust state estimation in the presence of heavy-tailed measurement noises and outliers. The multi-kernel correntropy loss is reformulated via half-quadratic (HQ) duality into a quadratic form with auxiliary weights, enabling efficient Kalman-like recursions. We establish a rigorous variational Bayesian framework under the mean-field assumption, deriving closed-form coordinate ascent variational inference updates for joint state and kernel width estimation. To enable online implementation, AMCKF is then proposed as an efficient single-pass approximation of the exact coordinate ascent variational inference (CAVI), where the state and auxiliary variables are updated iteratively via IRLS, followed by a closed-form kernel width update. A forgetting mechanism is designed to prevent over-confidence and ensure long-term adaptability. Simulations on target tracking and IMU attitude estimation demonstrate that the proposed AMCKF significantly outperforms standard KF, VBKF, Student's t-based KF, and fixed-kernel MKCKF in scenarios with sparse outliers, heavy-tailed noise, and mixed disturbances, while maintaining optimal performance under Gaussian conditions.
