The double Heston model is a widely used stochastic volatility model in financial mathematics, particularly for option pricing, risk management, and volatility surface calibration. This paper investigates the joint estimation of the drift parameters for an ergodic extension of the double Heston model. We first establish stochastic properties of the underlying process, including the existence of a stationary distribution and ergodicity. We then study maximum likelihood and conditional least squares estimators based on continuous-time observations. For both estimation procedures, we establish consistency and asymptotic normality under suitable ergodicity conditions.
