Nonparametric expectile regression methods face new challenges from streaming data, where retaining the full dataset for batch processing becomes increasingly impractical. In this paper, we develop a scalable nonparametric expectile regression framework for varying coefficient models. We estimate the coefficient functions online via functional stochastic gradient descent with Polyak--Ruppert averaging in a product reproducing kernel Hilbert space, requiring only gradient information and one pass over the data. For this online estimator, we establish non-asymptotic error bounds, prove its optimal nonparametric convergence rate under the proposed step-size schemes, and derive its Berry--Esseen bound for pointwise asymptotic normality. To perform online inference for the varying coefficient expectile regression models, we propose a multiplier bootstrap procedure that perturbs the stochastic gradients with independent random weights and recursively constructs bootstrap trajectories. We establish the bootstrap consistency of the online inference procedure, yielding asymptotically valid pointwise confidence intervals. Simulation studies and a real data application confirm the performance of the proposed methods.
