Under multivariate normality, we establish exact Geary-type expectation identities for Mardia's sample skewness and kurtosis. A polar decomposition of the centered Gaussian data matrix shows that each of these statistics is independent of the sample covariance matrix. This independence allows the moments of each statistic to be expressed as ratios of expectations involving polynomial numerators and powers of the determinant of the sample covariance matrix. The identities hold in arbitrary dimension $p$. To illustrate their use, we give exact expressions for the variance of Mardia's sample skewness and the third central moment of Mardia's sample kurtosis for $p=2$ and $p=3$. As an application of the exact variance formulas, we construct a moment-matched chi-square approximation to the null distribution of Mardia's sample skewness and compare its performance with Mardia's classical and mean-corrected approximations.
