We derive explicit expressions for the mixed moments of the scaled Dirichlet distribution in terms of the Lauricella hypergeometric function of type $D$. The relationship with Carlson's multivariate $R$-function is stated, and the convergence conditions required for the series derivation are made explicit. The general expressions yield means, second moments and covariances, with Gauss and Appell hypergeometric representations in the bivariate and trivariate cases. Method-of-moments and maximum-likelihood formulations are presented, together with simulation summaries and an illustrative comparison of scaled Dirichlet and ordinary Dirichlet fits to compositional futures-return data.
