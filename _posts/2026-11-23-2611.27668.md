Natural spaces support health and well-being, but increasing recreational use can put pressure on ecologically sensitive sites. Understanding when, where and why people visit is important for planning, site management and evaluating interventions. Building on work by the Office for National Statistics, we developed statistical and machine learning models to predict visitor numbers at points located in natural spaces. We trained on automated people counter data across a range of green and blue spaces in England and Wales and tested the models' ability to generalise to unseen counter locations. Our models offer a scalable approach for predicting visitor numbers in natural spaces, reducing reliance on surveys and physical counters.
