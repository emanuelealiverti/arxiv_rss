We study variable screening for high-frequency continuous-time regression, targeting the support of the spot regression coefficient. Under coordinatewise truncation and finite-variation jumps, uniform entrywise bounds for local correlation estimates yield the rate $\{(\log p)/n\}^{\alpha/(2\alpha+1)}$ under a local averaged $\alpha$-H\"older condition, up to confidence and grid factors. These bounds imply sure screening for the marginal support. To retain the regression support under local dependence, we propose partial-residual spot iterative screening with pathwise Lepski adaptation. Under sparse eigenvalues and standardized beta-min, the procedure has the sure screening property and requires only correlation columns associated with the current active set. A list-size minimax lower bound applies to both support targets and matches the localization and multiplicity order of the marginal-screening bound when $\log(p/d)\asymp\log p$. Simulations and a high-frequency factor application illustrate the procedures.
