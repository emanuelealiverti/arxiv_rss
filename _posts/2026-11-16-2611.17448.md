This article studies the envelope model for enhanced gene expression analysis using GTEx data. The study compares this advanced statistical approach with standard multivariate linear regression, demonstrating improved capacity for identifying significant genes and enhanced estimation efficiency. By partitioning response variation into material and immaterial components, the envelope model reduces estimation noise, leading to stronger statistical signals and smaller standard errors. These findings, as demonstrated in our tissue-specific and gene-group anal- yses, show that the envelope model can pick up on important biological signals that traditional methods tend to miss. This makes it a useful tool for genomic studies and GTEx data analysis.
