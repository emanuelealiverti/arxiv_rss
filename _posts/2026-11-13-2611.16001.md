We introduce the Proper Random Walk (PRW) family of spline models with a particular focus on the Second-Order Proper Random Walk (PRW2), a proper stationary alternative to the standard random walk of order 2 (RW2) spline. The PRW2 retains the computational strengths and spline-like smoothing of the RW2 while providing uniform marginal variance and stable uncertainty behavior. The PRW2 model is governed by a range parameter that controls correlation decay: in the infinite-range limit, it recovers RW2. In addition to its discrete representation, we show that the PRW2 model admits a continuous-time formulation as the critically damped Mat\'ern model with smoothness $\nu=3/2$, which provides an interpretable parameterization and enables construction through a Galerkin finite-element approximation on general grids. To mitigate edge distortions on bounded domains, we propose a local boundary correction that enforces the stationary joint law while preserving sparsity and $O(n)$ factorization. Finally, we develop penalized complexity priors for the model hyperparameters, enabling principled shrinkage toward the RW2 limit. The model is applied to simulated and real data to illustrate its properties and advantages.
