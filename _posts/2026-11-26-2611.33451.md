Existing regression-based methods for functional predictors often overlook right-censored competing risks outcomes, limiting their applicability in many biomedical studies. To address this gap, this article proposes a functional direct binomial model for right-censored competing risks data, where a binary outcome indicates the occurrence of an event of interest before a fixed time horizon. The model enables straightforward prediction of the cumulative incidence function, can be fitted using standard, scalable algorithms for large-scale studies, and has broad applications to functional predictors, such as wearable device data. We establish large-sample theory to facilitate inference, including hypothesis testing and the construction of confidence intervals and bands for both scalar coefficients and functional parameters. The methodology is further extended to accommodate multiple functional biomarkers and to handle missing failure causes. Simulation studies confirm that the proposed method provides accurate estimation and valid inference. We illustrate the approach with an application to modeling time to advanced chronic kidney disease (CKD) in the presence of the competing risk of death, leveraging continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) data and baseline covariates. The analysis shows that CGM is both a significant predictor and a viable alternative to the traditional HbA1c biomarker for CKD.
