I congratulate the authors on their contribution, which addresses important challenges in understanding posterior distributions in Bayesian mixture models. As underlined by the authors, the Bayesian framework has several advantages, yet label-switching, identifiability issues, and the trans-dimensional nature of parameter spaces make exploiting the Bayesian approach and understanding the resulting posterior difficult in practice. The paper offers clear explanations and a simple, intuitive procedure to address this by clustering the Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) draws of component-specific parameters. It is clearly illustrated on several examples.
