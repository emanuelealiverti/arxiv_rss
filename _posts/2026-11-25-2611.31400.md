This article proposes an exact Bayesian formulation of the B-FBST Student's t-test applied to the one-sample Student's t-test. The method relies on constructing an exact posterior distribution of the standardized parameter, then calculating the e-value to measure the evidence against the null hypothesis. To improve the stability of the procedure, posterior densities obtained from bootstrap samples are aggregated using random weights. A Monte Carlo simulation study was then conducted to compare the B-FBST Student's t-test with the classical Student's t-test and the bootstrap Student's t-test. The results, obtained from 100 Monte Carlo replications and 200 bootstrap replications, show that the B-FBST Student's t-test exhibits favorable overall performance, with an average of 71.0% correct decisions, compared to 65.5% for the classical test and 70.7% for the bootstrap test. The proposed method thus appears to be a robust alternative, particularly in several situations where the classical assumptions of the Student's t-test are compromised.
