To jointly identify temporal regimes and row-column structure in matrix-valued time series, we propose a hidden Markov model (HMM) with Gaussian co-clustering distributions. A single latent regime governs each matrix. Conditional on the regime, row and column memberships define Gaussian blocks, each with its own mean and variance. Membership probabilities and block parameters are shared across occurrences of a regime, while individual memberships may vary between time points.
