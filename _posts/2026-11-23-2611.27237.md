We study Parareal approximations of stochastic \(L^2\)-boosting, reusing the learner draws of the sequential algorithm in the fine propagators. We establish mean-square error bounds whose order improves with each correction, characterize the limiting distributions of normalized errors, including non-Gaussian components and the effects of coarse discretization, and derive sufficient conditions for preserving the statistical limit laws and validation-based stopping behavior of the sequential algorithm. Numerical experiments with sample-dependent coordinate and nonlinear residual-fitting learners examine the predicted convergence rates and limiting distributions, while computational benchmarks assess the efficiency of the method relative to sequential implementations.
