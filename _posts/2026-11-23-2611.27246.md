Mass spectrometry (MS)-based proteomics is essential for comprehensive protein characterization, yet it relies on multistep computational workflows that typically demand tedious manual parameter tuning and complex software integration. Here, we present MSRyzen, an agent-driven framework that provides automated parameter optimization and extensible tool integration across bottom-up and top-down proteomics workflows. MSRyzen translates natural-language prompts into directed acyclic workflow graphs, optimizes analytical parameters guided by quantitative metrics and user-defined objectives, and incorporates new third-party software through standardized callable interfaces. Across four public top-down datasets, automated parameter optimization increased proteoform identifications by 3.6% to 15.8% compared to default settings at a 1% false discovery rate. To ensure transparent data interpretation, an interactive visualization interface coordinates identification metrics with peak-level spectral evidence and sequence coverage. Integrating adaptive tuning with multi-tiered spectral inspection, MSRyzen delivers an end-to-end, fully automated framework, dramatically reducing the operational barrier to modern mass spectrometry data analysis.
