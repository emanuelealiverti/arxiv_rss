We study the iterative proportional fitting procedure (IPFP) under multiple pushforward constraints, motivated by model calibration. The goal is to find a distribution on model parameters whose pushforward by models matches data distributions. The classic convergence result of IPFP under marginal constraints establish convergence of IPFP under suitable regularity conditions and develop a variant of sequential importance sampling to approximate the iterative procedure. We also propose a related procedure for constructing a high probability parameter region. Simulation studies illustrate the performance of these methods. Finally, we apply IPFP to COVID19 ensemble forecasting. Variation of Covid strains across regions can lead to variation in surge rates and thus affect forecasting accuracy. To capture the variation, we estimate a probability distribution of epidemiological model parameters using early stage surge data from an ensemble of regions. We then propagate the distribution through the model to forecast both the ensemble's overall trend and its variability.
