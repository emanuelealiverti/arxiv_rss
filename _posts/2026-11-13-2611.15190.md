We develop inference for structural patterns in the temporal association of object-valued time series. Our framework is built on pairwise lag profiles, which record the expected value of a chosen pairwise function at each lag and serve as a unified inferential target. We consider three representative structural features: whether a profile reaches stability beyond a finite lag, remains constant over time, and displays geometric regularity at short lags. To assess these properties, we design statistical tests calibrated by a common block multiplier bootstrap that accommodates serial dependence and the overlap inherent in lagged pairs. Gaussian approximation results establish bootstrap validity for asymptotic size control, while suitable signal conditions guarantee consistency against the corresponding alternatives. Finite-sample performance is examined through simulations in both Euclidean and density-valued settings. Applications to electricity load curves and stock-return distributions illustrate how the proposed procedures characterize association across lags.
