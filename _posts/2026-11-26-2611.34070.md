Ecological inference aims to estimate the bivariate association structure between a pair of variables from the row and column totals of a set of contingency tables. The paper introduces a new approach based on minimizing the Cressie--Read power divergence, applied to the estimation of vote transitions between two elections. It also outlines a Fisher scoring algorithm and derives the asymptotic distribution of the estimator.
