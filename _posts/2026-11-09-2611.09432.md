This paper proposes a hybrid model for analyzing zero-inflated proportion data by distinguishing two types of zeros. The first type arises from sampling variability, whereas the second reflects intrinsically unsuitable covariates. Accordingly, the hybrid model combines a regression component for suitable observations with an estimator of the suitability region that identifies unsuitable covariates. Using a debiasing technique, we establish the consistency of both the regression and classification components, together with the asymptotic normality of the regression coefficient and a convergence rate for the support estimator. Comprehensive simulation studies verify our theoretical findings. We further apply the model to two real datasets, where it outperforms most competing methods.
