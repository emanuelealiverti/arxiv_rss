Small area estimates of counts are often required to conform exactly to reliable aggregates at a higher geographic level. Often the area-level model is specified on a transformed (nonlinear) scale such as a log scale. Specifying the small area model on the log-scale is common practice for positive responses. A prominent example is the production of the Small Area Income and Poverty Estimates (SAIPE) by the US Census Bureau. When the model is specified in a transformed scale, the benchmarking constraints, which are linear in the area means, become nonlinear in terms of the model parameters. We develop a new class of priors for Bayesian small area models under strict external benchmarking constraints when the small area model is specified in a transformed scale, and the model parameters belong to a Riemannian manifold due to the nonlinear benchmarking constraints. The prior is fully supported on the constrained parameter set, the manifold defined by the benchmarking constraints, and thereby provides a proper constrained posterior that is also supported on the restricted set. This facilitates full Bayesian analysis of the problem without the requirement of post-hoc modification to the posterior to enforce the benchmarking constraints.
