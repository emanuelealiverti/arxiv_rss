There are many misunderstandings about the term and interpretation of uniqueness in multivariate curve resolution tasks. In CAC2026 Tarragona, it turned out that even mathematicians do not properly construe Manne's theorems. So we decided to provide this re-tuned summary, using several original quotes, their explanations, and newly created illustrative figures to get the points across.
