Decomposable graphical models combine closed-form maximum likelihood estimation with local model comparison, which makes structure inference practical with many variables. The R package GraphInfer provides one likelihood framework for decomposable models of continuous, discrete and mixed data, the last as conditional Gaussian models. Structure inference ranges from exact minimum forests and stepwise search to stochastic global search. Nested models are compared by likelihood-ratio tests whose null distributions are exact in finite samples: conditionally on the discrete data for edges with a continuous endpoint, and conditionally on the discrete margins of the smaller model for edges between discrete variables. Missing values are handled by EM, by structural EM, which reuses complete-data searches through a weighted pseudo-sample of expected sufficient statistics, and by multiple imputation; selected models are fit to the observed-data likelihood. The package also treats the graphical models induced by irreversible event accumulation on trees: a search over the compatible graphs whose tree moves overcome an obstruction to single-edge moves, fitting of the full accumulation model with measurement noise and bootstrap tests of the accumulation hypothesis. GraphInfer is an independent, pure R successor to the archived package gRapHD. The implementation is validated against independent references, and simulation studies inform its defaults.
