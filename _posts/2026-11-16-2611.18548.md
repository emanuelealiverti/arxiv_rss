We study the effect of lifting on continuous-time Markov processes with potentially subgeometric convergence rates. Using a series of weak Poincar\'e-type inequalities, we derive a bound on the maximum acceleration that can be obtained through lifting. We present several examples to explore the sharpness of our derived bound, where the resulting bound has the same square-root order as in the spectral gap setting. Our results provide a framework for understanding the potential and limitations of non-reversible lifting beyond geometric convergence.
