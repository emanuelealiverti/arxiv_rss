We study long-memory continuous-time moving-average processes driven by a Levy process and observed at random renewal times. The sampling scheme introduces an additional source of randomness through irregular observation times. We establish the asymptotic behaviour of normalized partial sums under both finite- and infinite-mean renewal sampling.
