We propose Fr\'echet oblique forests (FOF) for non-Euclidean regression with predictors and responses in metric spaces. An Alexandrov inner-product score defines directional splits in metric predictor spaces and recovers ordinary oblique splits for Euclidean predictors. Candidate splits are evaluated through pairwise response distances, avoiding repeated Fr\'echet mean computations during split search. Forest predictions minimize a weighted Fr\'echet criterion obtained by averaging leaf weights across trees. For compatible tree rules, we establish integrated consistency and polynomial risk bounds under explicit approximation and entropy conditions. Simulations with matrices, distributions, spherical data and mixed predictors, together with an application to taxi-flow networks, demonstrate competitive predictive performance.
