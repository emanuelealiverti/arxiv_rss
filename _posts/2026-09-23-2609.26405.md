Longitudinal data, often in the form of panel data, are commonly encountered in the pharmaceutical industry. However it is not always possible to fit an unstructured covariance matrix when using the Mixed Model for Repeated Measures (MMRM). Here we argue that high-order autoregressive models provide a useful framework when more general correlation structures, such as the unstructured and/or Toeplitz, are unfeasible. The autoregressive model of order one is often suggested but, in our experience, higher orders are seldom considered. We present the case for using autoregressive models, with potentially high orders, as a flexible framework to model correlations in panel data, when more general structures are hard to identify.
